🌱 Photosynthesis Game

Learn how plants make food using sunlight, water, and CO₂.

  • Plants absorb CO₂ from the air and water from the soil.
  • Chlorophyll in leaves captures sunlight to convert CO₂ & H₂O into Glucose and O₂.
  • Glucose is used by the plant for energy and growth.
  • Oxygen is released into the atmosphere as a byproduct.
  • Try to catch the right molecules and complete the photosynthesis equation!

🌱 Photosynthesis Game
